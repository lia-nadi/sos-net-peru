### EU-AIACT-ANEXOIII · art. Anexo III.2, III.5(d) y art. 14(1) · Alto riesgo: infraestructura digital crítica y despacho de emergencias; supervisión humana

- Efecto: **obliga** · Texto: literal · Confiabilidad: T

> Annex III.2: AI systems intended to be used as safety components in the management and operation of critical digital infrastructure (...). Annex III.5(d): AI systems intended to evaluate and classify emergency calls by natural persons or to be used to dispatch, or to establish priority in the dispatching of, emergency first response services (...). Art. 14(1): High-risk AI systems shall be designed and developed in such a way (...) that they can be effectively overseen by natural persons during the period in which they are in use.
